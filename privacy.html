Hand-Me-Down Solitaire is published by Mahesh Kommu. This policy explains local saves, the Google advertising services, Apple purchases and information you choose to send to support.

Local game progress

Your rounds, progress, unlocked themes, preferences and tutorial completion are saved on your device. The app does not require a game account or send these saves to a developer server. There is no automatic cross-device sync or separate gameplay analytics service.

The bundled tutorial video plays offline and does not contact a video-hosting service. Support cannot automatically access your locally saved progress.

Google AdMob advertising

The iOS app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). World 1 is ad-free. Later, a full-screen static or video interstitial may appear at scheduled transitions between completed rounds. No ads appear during gameplay or the tutorial. An active Remove Ads entitlement prevents ad requests and display.

The app disables publisher first-party IDs and publisher personalization and does not request permission to access Apple’s advertising identifier. This does not make advertising data-free. Google’s SDKs may process your IP address and approximate location inferred from it, app- or device-bounded identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, product interactions, crash logs, performance information and other diagnostic data. Google uses advertising-related data to provide and measure ads, prevent fraud, perform analytics and operate or improve its services.

UMP manages the privacy messages and choices required for your region. Where required, consent eligibility is checked before requesting an ad, and Ad privacy choices is available in Settings. Choosing not to consent does not remove access to the game. Depending on eligibility, limited ads may be available or ads may be skipped. Retention and processing by Google are governed by Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Learn more about data processed by Google Mobile Ads. We do not add mediation networks, a separate analytics SDK or a developer advertising profile.

Apple purchases

Apple handles optional one-time purchases (Remove Ads and expedition packs), payments and refunds. The app uses StoreKit to verify entitlements on your device, restore them and respond to revoked purchases. The developer does not receive your card details or send receipts or transaction identifiers to a custom verification server. See Apple’s App Store privacy information.

When you contact support

If you email samvaiotmp@icloud.com, we receive your email address, message and any attachments or diagnostic details you choose to include. We use them to respond, investigate and resolve your request, and keep them only as needed for that purpose or legal obligations. You may request deletion of correspondence. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information.

Your choices and retention

You can play offline, manage consent through the available ad privacy controls, purchase Remove Ads or delete the app’s local data. Deleting the app may permanently remove local progress. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple purchase or delete information already processed by Apple or Google.

The game is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. Contact support if you believe a child has sent personal information so we can address it.

Google and Apple may process information in countries other than your own and retain it according to their policies. Use their privacy controls for information held by those services; contact us for information you sent directly to support.

These support pages

These static pages have no added analytics scripts, advertising SDK or contact form. GitHub Pages hosts them and processes request metadata, including IP addresses, for delivery and security. See GitHub’s privacy statement. External links lead to services with their own privacy practices.

Questions or requests

Contact Mahesh Kommu at samvaiotmp@icloud.com about this policy or support correspondence. We will post policy changes here with an updated date. Visit Support for help with the game and purchases.
