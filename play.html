A different kind of card puzzle

The next decision starts underneath.

  1. Find a match

    To move between occupied columns, match number badges one apart before the move.

  2. Uncover and exchange

    If the move uncovers a card in the source column, those two cards exchange number badges. The destination card keeps its badge.

  3. Bring a family home

    Collect each creature family in number order, starting with 1. Collecting never exchanges badges.

A short interactive tutorial teaches the moves. Holding spaces and later variations add more ways to plan.
