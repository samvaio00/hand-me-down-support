Learn by playing

The first launch opens a short interactive tutorial. Replay it from Settings → Replay interactive tutorial without changing your active round. A separate 28-second tutorial video in Settings starts only when you press Play.

Bring each family home in number order, starting with 1. A transfer between columns is checked using the badges before the move: the two numbers must differ by one. The moving card then exchanges badges with the card it uncovers at the source. Collecting never exchanges badges. Use Read the rules for the full explanation and any variations in the current round.

A round feels stuck?

Hint explains a useful next move. Undo and Restart are unlimited. Optional powers are never required to solve a generated deal. If a hint, rule or move seems wrong, report the round and what you tried before restarting.

The Journey has eight worlds with ten new completions each. Opening a world unlocks its theme for use anywhere. After all eight, Endless opens with random scenery by default. Settings lets you pick an unlocked theme or return to Random.

Keep your progress safe

Progress and preferences are stored on your device. Do not delete the app or reset its data to troubleshoot a problem before contacting support. Deleting the app may remove saved progress.

There is no game account or automatic cross-device sync. If progress looks wrong, avoid deleting or resetting the app before contacting support.

Remove Ads and restore a purchase

Remove Ads is the only paid option: a permanent, one-time purchase with a US base price of $2.99. Apple shows your local price before purchase. There are no subscriptions, paid worlds or paid powers.

If ads return after reinstalling or changing devices, use the Apple Account that bought Remove Ads and select Restore Purchases in Settings. Apple handles billing and refunds; visit Apple’s purchase support. Never send support your payment details or Apple password.

Ads stay between rounds

World 1 has no ads. Worlds 2–3 allow an ad after every three completed rounds; World 4 onward and Endless allow one after every two. Static or video ads can appear only when moving on between rounds. There are no banners or ads during a move or tutorial. If an ad is unavailable, play continues.

When the consent service requires it, Ad privacy choices appears in Settings. See the privacy policy for the advertising data and controls.
